## The divergence stance

This repository started as the optimizer-seed and is expected to **drift** from
it: your lessons graduate into its best practices, your instruments accumulate
in your labs, your edits reshape its skills. That divergence is the point — do
not treat upstream as a source of truth to stay synced with.

To see what upstream changed since planting: diff against the version recorded
above. Adopt what's useful; ignore the rest. There is no update mechanism by
design.

## Origins

Designed from a comparative study of four working Coworld optimizer
repositories (player_labs, optimizer-skills, cogamer, co-gas), synthesizing
their shared kernel and best individual capabilities into one cohesive system.
Design documents live in the optim-compare project alongside this seed's
upstream.
